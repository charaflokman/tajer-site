01

A day in the shop

From opening the door to closing the till, Tajer is with you at every step.

  1. 08:00

    Open the shift

    Count the cash in the drawer and type the amount. The program keeps count all day.

  2. 09:30

    The goods arrive

    Receive the delivery from the supplier's PDF or Excel invoice, with quantities and expiry dates.

  3. 11:00

    Rush at the till

    Scan the barcode and the price shows. Everything on the keyboard, the receipt prints at once.

  4. 13:00

    A customer buys on credit

    Pick the customer and the amount goes into their book. Each has a limit and a printed statement.

  5. 17:00

    A customer returns an item

    Find the receipt and pick the item. Stock and drawer correct themselves.

  6. 20:00

    Close the day

    The day report in one press: sales, credit, cash in the drawer. Then a backup.

Requirements

  • Windows 7 or later PC, 2 GB RAM, any dual-core CPU.
  • ESC/POS receipt printer, 58 or 80 mm (optional).
  • USB barcode scanner or phone camera (optional).
  • Android 5 or later phone (optional).

Install

  1. Run the installer and keep the folder C:\RetailPOS (a name in Latin letters).
  2. Allow the firewall rule if you want to pair a phone.
  3. On first start: language, shop with NIF and RC, backup password, administrator.
